Investing in & Encouraging the Arts in Calvert County

Creating & maintaining space, support, encouragement & accessibility that allows every person to be creative.

The Arts Council of Calvert County is a nonprofit dedicated to encouraging, supporting, and promoting the arts across Calvert County, Maryland — through galleries, classes, grants, and community programs.

Our Home

Kocen Arts Center

Located at 22 Duke Street, Prince Frederick, the Kocen Arts Center is the creative heart of Calvert County — offering art classes, open studio time, theater rehearsals, and live demonstrations throughout the year.

The Center is home to workshops for every skill level, from first-time painters to seasoned potters, and serves as a rehearsal and performance space for partner theater groups.
