The Calvert County Barn Quilt Trail

A driving trail of 27 hand-painted quilt squares mounted on barns, businesses, and buildings across Calvert County — celebrating Maryland's quilting heritage and rural folk art.

About the Trail

A Patchwork Across the County

Calvert County is Maryland's smallest county in land area, at just 213 square miles, located 35 miles south of Washington, D.C. Along its quiet roads, 27 painted quilt squares mark barns and buildings, each pattern chosen to reflect the history, family, or character of its site.

How to Follow the Trail

1

Get the Map

Pick up a printed trail map at the Kocen Arts Center or download the route before you set out.

2

Drive at Your Own Pace

Squares are spread across the county — plan for a half-day or full-day scenic drive.

3

Share Your Journey

Tag @calvertartscouncil and use the trail hashtag when you photograph each stop.

Support the Trail

The Barn Quilt Trail is maintained by the Arts Council of Calvert County with support from local landowners, volunteers, and the Maryland State Arts Council. New squares are added as sponsorships and locations become available.
